## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Cohesive deep screen mobile layout
Primary and nested screens SHALL use consistent safe-area spacing, readable section rhythm and reachable controls at 320px and 390px widths, including keyboard-open and long localized content.

#### Scenario: Open a deep editor on a small phone
- **WHEN** the editor is displayed with the keyboard open
- **THEN** its controls remain scrollable and reachable without overflow or excessive fixed empty space.
